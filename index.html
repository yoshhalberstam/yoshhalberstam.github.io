Yosh Halberstam

Economist · Washington, DC

I study behavior and how information, incentives and institutions shape it. My tools are causal inference and careful measurement, applied to administrative, survey, social-network and audio data.

I was an assistant professor of economics at the University of Toronto for eleven years and a senior financial economist at the FDIC's Center for Financial Research for four. I now write expert economics and finance problems and evaluate the reasoning of AI models.

PhD and MA, Northwestern University · BA, Hebrew University of Jerusalem
